How to Get from Bogotá to Guatapé and El Peñol Rock: Real Options

Guatapé and El Peñol Rock are an Antioquia classic. Many people search for how to go from Bogotá, so here are the real distances to help you plan properly.

Updated: October 14, 2026 · VanTour Bogotá

Quick facts

Distance from Bogotá
~400 km (~250 mi) by road
Overland time
8 to 10 h each way
From Medellín
~2 to 2.5 h
Recommended
A 2 to 3 day trip

Let's be clear about the timing

Guatapé is in Antioquia, near Medellín. From Bogotá it is about 400 km and the road trip takes many hours each way. It is not a day trip from Bogotá: it is planned as part of a longer journey.

Three ways to plan it

  • Flight Bogotá–Medellín + van tour: the fastest option. You land in Medellín and we do the Guatapé day with private transport.
  • Overland route over 2–3 days: road trip with stops and a night on the way or in Guatapé; ideal for groups who want to see the scenery.
  • Medellín–Guatapé–Coffee Region circuit: a popular combination covering several regions on one route.

What to do in Guatapé

  • Climb the steps of El Peñol Rock and see the reservoir from the top.
  • Stroll through the town known for its colorful baseboard façades.
  • Take a boat ride on the reservoir.
