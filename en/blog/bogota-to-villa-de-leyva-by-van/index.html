How to Get from Bogotá to Villa de Leyva by Private Van (2026 Guide)

Villa de Leyva is Boyacá's most visited colonial town and one of the favorite getaways from Bogotá. This guide explains how long it takes, the best way to get there and how to plan your day.

Updated: October 14, 2026 · VanTour Bogotá

Quick facts

Distance
~165 km (~100 mi)
Travel time
3 to 4 h each way
Best for
A long day trip or 2 days
Best time
Year-round; avoid heavy rain

How long is the drive from Bogotá to Villa de Leyva?

It is roughly 165 km and takes 3 to 4 hours each way, depending on traffic leaving Bogotá (especially Friday afternoons and holidays) and road conditions. Leaving before 7:00 a.m. is the best way to avoid delays.

Private van, bus or rental car?

  • Private van: leave when you want, stop where you like (Ráquira, Pozos Azules, viewpoints) and skip the timetables. It is the most comfortable option for families and groups of 4 to 14.
  • Intercity bus: cheap, but you arrive at the terminal and depend on schedules and local transport to reach the sights.
  • Rental car: doable, but the road has mountain stretches and parking in town on weekends is difficult.

What to see in Villa de Leyva

  • Plaza Mayor: one of the largest cobblestone squares in Colombia.
  • Pozos Azules and the Fossil Museum: landscapes and fossils millions of years old nearby.
  • Ráquira: the pottery town with colorful façades, on the way there or back.
  • Vineyards and country houses: tastings and lunch with a view.

Suggested one-day itinerary

Depart Bogotá at 6:30 a.m. → arrive around 10:00 a.m. → walk the town and have lunch → visit Pozos Azules or Ráquira → leave at 4:30 p.m. → back in Bogotá at night. For a more relaxed trip, stay one night in town and return the next day.

Practical tips

  • Bring cash: many small shops don't take cards.
  • Weather shifts: cool mornings and strong midday sun. Pack a light jacket and sunscreen.
  • On long weekends the road gets congested; book your transport in advance.

Frequently asked questions

Can I do Villa de Leyva as a day trip from Bogotá?

Yes, if you leave early, but the round trip adds 6 to 8 hours of driving. To enjoy it without rushing, we recommend one night.
