Guatavita Lake: How to Get There from Bogotá and What to Expect

Laguna de Guatavita is the birthplace of the El Dorado legend and one of the shortest and most fascinating excursions from Bogotá. Here is what you need to visit without hassle.

Updated: October 14, 2026 · VanTour Bogotá

Quick facts

Distance
~75 km (~47 mi)
Travel time
1.5 to 2 h each way
Best for
Half or full day
Altitude
Over 3,000 m (9,800 ft)

Why is it so famous?

According to Muisca tradition, this lake was the setting of the ceremony in which the new chieftain covered himself in gold dust and offered treasures to the gods. That story became the El Dorado legend that drew explorers from around the world.

How to get there from Bogotá

You drive north through Sesquilé and Guatavita. The trip takes 1.5 to 2 hours depending on traffic. With a private van you can combine the lake with the town of Guatavita or the Zipaquirá Salt Cathedral on the same day.

What the visit is like

  • Access to the reserve is usually guided and in groups, with daily limits; confirm schedules and availability before you go.
  • The hike includes uphill stretches; take it slowly because of the altitude.
  • Bring warm, waterproof clothing: páramo weather changes fast.

Recommended combinations

  • Guatavita Lake + the new town of Guatavita (lunch and main square).
  • Guatavita Lake + Zipaquirá Salt Cathedral.

Frequently asked questions

How long is the full excursion?

Between 5 and 8 hours including transfers, the hike and lunch, depending on what you combine.

Do I need to book the entrance?

Entry to the lake has quotas and schedules set by the site's administration. We recommend confirming availability in advance; we can guide you when you request your quote.

Do you pick up at the hotel?

Yes, we pick you up and drop you back at your hotel or accommodation in Bogotá.
