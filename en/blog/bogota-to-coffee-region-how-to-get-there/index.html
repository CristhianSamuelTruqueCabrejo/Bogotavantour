How to Get from Bogotá to the Coffee Region by Private Van: Times and Routes

Colombia's Coffee Region —Salento, Filandia, the Cocora Valley— is one of the most requested destinations by travelers. Here is how to get there from Bogotá and how many days to set aside.

Updated: October 14, 2026 · VanTour Bogotá

Quick facts

Distance
~300 to 350 km depending on destination
Overland time
7 to 9 h each way
Suggested length
3 to 5 days
Best season
December–March and July–August

How long is the trip from Bogotá to the Coffee Region?

By road it can take 7 to 9 hours each way, since the route crosses the Central Andes with slow stretches and heavy trucks. That is why it is not a same-day round trip: plan 3 to 5 days.

Private van or flight

  • Flight to Pereira or Armenia: saves hours on the road; we then move you around the region by private van.
  • Van from Bogotá: ideal for groups and families who want stops along the way, no luggage limits and the comfort of not driving.

What to visit

  • Cocora Valley: giant wax palms and mountain hikes.
  • Salento and Filandia: colorful-street towns with crafts and viewpoints.
  • Coffee farms: tours showing the coffee process, with tastings.
  • Coffee Park or hot springs: options for families and relaxing.

How to organize it

A common plan: day 1 travel and overnight in the area; days 2 and 3 local tours (Cocora, a farm, towns); final day return. We quote the full route with a driver throughout the trip.

Frequently asked questions

Does the driver stay with us the whole trip?

Yes, on multi-day trips the driver accompanies you on all tours.

Can you pick us up at Pereira or Armenia airport?

Yes, we arrange pickup at those airports if you prefer to fly.

How many days do you recommend?

At least 3 days to enjoy it unhurried; 4 or 5 if you want to combine several towns and activities.
