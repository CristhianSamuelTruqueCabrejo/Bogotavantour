Zipaquirá Salt Cathedral from Bogotá: How to Get There and How Long It Takes

The Salt Cathedral is a church built inside a mine and one of the most popular plans near Bogotá. It works well as a half-day trip or paired with Guatavita.

Updated: October 14, 2026 · VanTour Bogotá

Quick facts

Distance
~50 km (~31 mi)
Travel time
1 to 1.5 h each way
Visit length
1.5 to 2 h
Best for
Half day

What is the Salt Cathedral?

It is an underground church carved into a salt mine, with tunnels, monumental crosses and light displays. It is one of Colombia's tourist icons, located in the town of Zipaquirá, north of Bogotá.

How to get there from Bogotá

  • Private van: we pick you up at your hotel with no transfers; ideal for families or if you're carrying luggage.
  • Tourist train or buses: public options exist, but with fixed schedules and waiting times.

Leaving early helps you avoid lines and return traffic, especially on weekends and holidays.

How long is the visit?

The mine tour takes 1.5 to 2 hours. Add time to stroll the town square and have lunch. Overall, plan on a 5 to 6 hour half-day including transport.

Combine it with another plan

  • Zipaquirá + Guatavita Lake on a long day.
  • Zipaquirá + Nemocón (a smaller salt mine) for a quieter option.

Frequently asked questions

Is it a good idea to go on weekends?

That is when it is busiest. If you can, go on a weekday or arrive first thing.

Is the entrance fee included in the transport?

The van service covers transport. Attraction tickets are paid separately; we'll guide you when quoting.

Can the driver wait while we visit?

Yes, the driver waits for you and takes you back to your hotel.
